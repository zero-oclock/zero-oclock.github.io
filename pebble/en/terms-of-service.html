2. Description of the service

Steward is a minimalist, offline-first personal bookkeeping app. It lets you record transactions quickly (including one-sentence and voice entry), review spending with charts, set budgets with overrun reminders, and manage recurring bills. Core bookkeeping, data, and reminders operate fully offline; the app connects to the network only for purchasing or restoring Steward Pro.

3. Free vs. Pro

The core features are free. Advanced features require Steward Pro.

  • Free: quick entry, one-sentence entry, transaction list, basic charts, budgets, overrun reminders, one theme, and the default language.
  • Pro: the full detailed-entry suite (multiple accounts, tags, reimbursement, AA split, split transactions, transfers, attachments), recurring bills with due reminders, liability / credit-card accounts, all themes plus custom themes, local automatic backup & data export, and advanced period-over-period charts.
