Steward — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 1, 2026

Overview

Steward is a minimalist, offline-first personal bookkeeping app. Our core promise is simple: your financial data stays on your device. This Privacy Policy explains what little data the app handles and how.

Core bookkeeping, data, and reminders operate 100% offline. The only time the app connects to the network is when you purchase or restore Steward Pro through Apple's StoreKit 2.

Information we do not collect

Steward does not collect, transmit, or store your personal information on any server. Specifically:

  • No account or sign-in system.
  • No device identifiers and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, and no third-party network SDK.
  • No backend server and no cloud database.
  • No collection of contacts, location, or browsing data.

Data you enter (stored locally)

The only data the app holds is what you enter yourself: transactions, categories, accounts, tags, budgets, recurring bills, quick templates, and any receipt or attachment photos. All of it is stored solely in the app's local sandbox (SwiftData) on your device, and never leaves your device as part of the app's operation.

How your data is stored

  • On-device: data is stored locally using SwiftData in the app sandbox.
  • No cloud sync: the app performs no cloud synchronization of any kind.
  • Device backups: if you enable iCloud Backup or a computer backup, the app's data may be included in that system-level backup. That is controlled entirely by you through your iOS settings, not by the app.
  • Local automatic backup (Pro): daily snapshots are kept in the app sandbox (the last 7 by default). These are also stored locally only.

Permissions we request and why

  • Microphone & Speech Recognition: used only for voice entry. Speech is transcribed on-device (Apple's SFSpeechRecognizer) and is not uploaded. If you decline, text entry still works normally.
  • Camera / Photos: used only to attach receipts or invoices; photos are stored locally. If you decline, attachments remain optional and everything else still works.
  • Notifications: used only for local reminders (budget overrun, recurring bill due, daily nudge). These are local notifications scheduled on your device — there is no server push. If you decline, in-app reminders still work.

Every permission is requested only when the feature is first used, with an explanation. Declining a permission degrades only that feature and never blocks core bookkeeping.

The only network activity: purchases and restore

The app's sole network connection is for purchasing or restoring Steward Pro via Apple's StoreKit 2. Payment processing, transactions, and receipts are handled entirely by Apple. Steward never sees, receives, or stores your payment information, credit-card details, or billing address. After a purchase, the entitlement is verified locally on your device and works offline.

Data retention and deletion

  • Your data remains on your device until you delete it.
  • Deleted entries are moved to a recycle bin and kept for 30 days, after which they are permanently erased. You can restore or permanently delete them at any time.
  • "Clear all data" permanently erases local data immediately.
  • Uninstalling the app removes the app and its sandbox data.

Your rights

You have full control over your data: you can export it (JSON/CSV), delete individual entries, or clear everything at any time. Because we hold no copy of your data on any server, there is nothing to request from us — your data is yours and stays with you.

Children's privacy

Steward is not directed at children under 13 (or the applicable age in your region), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since the app collects no personal information at all, there is no such data to collect.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted, and the "effective date" at the top reflects the latest version. We encourage you to review it periodically.
