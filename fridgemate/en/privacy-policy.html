FridgeMate — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 3, 2026

Overview

FridgeMate is a minimalist, offline-first fridge food manager and recipe recommendation app. Our core promise is simple: your food data stays on your device. This Privacy Policy explains what little data the app handles and how.

Food entry, management, reminders, and recipe recommendations operate 100% offline. The only time the app connects to the network is when you purchase or restore FridgeMate Pro through Apple's StoreKit 2.

Information we do not collect

FridgeMate does not collect, transmit, or store your personal information on any server. Specifically:

  • No account or sign-in system.
  • No device identifiers and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, and no third-party network SDK.
  • No backend server and no cloud database.
  • No collection of contacts, location, or browsing data.

Data you enter (stored locally)

The only data the app holds is what you enter yourself: food items, categories, expiration dates, consumption records, shopping lists, favorite foods, and custom recipes (including recipe photos you scan). All of it is stored solely in the app's local sandbox (SwiftData) on your device, and never leaves your device as part of the app's operation.

How your data is stored

  • On-device: data is stored locally using SwiftData in the app sandbox.
  • No cloud sync: the app performs no cloud synchronization of any kind.
  • Device backups: if you enable iCloud Backup or a computer backup, the app's data may be included in that system-level backup. That is controlled entirely by you through your iOS settings, not by the app.
  • Always yours to take: export and import are permanently free, so you can always take your data with you.

Permissions we request and why

  • Camera: used only to photograph and recognize recipe text, saving it as your custom recipe; the image is stored locally only.
  • Microphone & Speech Recognition: used only for one-sentence voice entry of food. Speech is transcribed on-device (Apple's SFSpeechRecognizer) and is not uploaded.
  • Notifications: used only for local expiry reminders scheduled on your device — there is no server push.

Every permission is requested only when the feature is first used, with an explanation. Declining a permission degrades only that feature and never blocks core usage.

The only network activity: purchases and restore

The app's sole network connection is for purchasing or restoring FridgeMate Pro via Apple's StoreKit 2. Payment processing, transactions, and receipts are handled entirely by Apple. FridgeMate never sees, receives, or stores your payment information, credit-card details, or billing address. After a purchase, the entitlement is verified locally on your device and works offline.

Data retention and deletion

  • Your data remains on your device until you delete it.
  • Deleted entries are removed immediately.
  • "Clear all data" permanently erases local data immediately.
  • Uninstalling the app removes the app and its sandbox data.

Your rights

You have full control over your data: you can export or import it at any time, free of charge, delete individual entries, or clear everything at once. You can also revoke the camera, microphone, speech recognition and notification permissions at any time in iOS Settings.

If you have a privacy request, question or complaint — for example about access to, correction or deletion of your data — contact us at selfiearts@163.com and we will respond within 30 days. We will help wherever we can, even though your data is stored only on your device. If you are in the EEA, the UK or another region with data protection law, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

Children's privacy

FridgeMate is not directed at children under 13 (or the applicable age in your region), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted, and the "effective date" at the top reflects the latest version. If a change materially affects your rights, we will notify you in the app before it takes effect. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
