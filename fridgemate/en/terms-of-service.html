2. Description of the service

FridgeMate is a minimalist, offline-first fridge food manager and recipe recommendation app. You can quickly record food with one sentence or voice entry, see expiry reminders, log consumption, maintain a shopping list, and get recipe recommendations based on what you have. Core features, data, and reminders operate fully offline; the app connects to the network only for purchasing or restoring FridgeMate Pro.

3. Free vs. Pro

The core features are free. Advanced features require FridgeMate Pro.

  • Free: one-sentence and voice entry, food management (up to 30 items), the shopping list, consumption records, and data export/import.
  • Pro: unlimited food items, full statistics, smart recipe recommendations, and other advanced features.
