Frequently asked questions

1. Where is my data stored? Does it sync between devices?

Everything you add — items, quantities, expiry dates, custom recipes — is stored only on this device, in the app's local storage. FridgeMate has no account and no server, so your data never leaves the device during normal use. It therefore does not appear automatically on another device; to move it, use export and import.

2. I reinstalled the app or changed devices — how do I get Pro back?

Pro is tied to your Apple ID, not to the device. On the Pro screen, tap Restore Purchases, and make sure the App Store is signed in with the same Apple ID you bought with. If Pro still does not unlock, send us your Apple receipt and the app version and we will sort it out.

3. How do I cancel the auto-renewable subscription?

  1. Open Settings on your iPhone or iPad.
  2. Tap your name at the top.
  3. Tap Subscriptions.
  4. Select FridgeMate, then tap Cancel Subscription.

You keep Pro until the end of the paid period. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription and does not stop billing.

4. How do I request a refund?

Payments are processed by Apple, and refund decisions are Apple's, not ours. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with the Apple ID used for the purchase, and submit the request there.

5. How do I delete my data?

Delete entries one by one in the app, or use the clear-all option to erase everything at once. Deleting the app also removes its local storage. We hold no copy on any server, so there is nothing on our side to delete — but if you would like a written confirmation, just email us.

6. I declined a permission. How do I turn it back on?

iOS only asks once, so after tapping “Don't Allow” the switch has to be flipped in Settings > FridgeMate. Camera is used for photographing recipe text, microphone and speech recognition for one-sentence voice entry, and notifications for expiry reminders. Each permission only affects its own feature; the rest of the app works either way.

7. Expiry reminders are not arriving.

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device; there is no server push. If nothing appears, check that notifications are allowed in Settings > FridgeMate > Notifications, that Do Not Disturb or a Focus mode is not silencing the app, and that the device date and time are correct.

8. Voice entry or recipe-text recognition gets it wrong.

Recognition runs on-device and depends on background noise, handwriting, lighting and language. For voice, say one short sentence at a time, starting with the ingredient name. For photos, avoid shadows and shoot the text straight on. If a specific word is consistently misread, tell us the word and we will look into it.

Before you email us

Including these details helps us solve the problem in one round:

  • FridgeMate version number
  • iOS version (Settings > General > About)
  • Device model
  • What you did and what you expected
  • A screenshot of the error or the wrong result

Please do not send photos that contain other people's private information — mask unrelated parts first.
