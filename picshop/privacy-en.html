PicShop Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 5, 2026  |  Last updated: November 5, 2026

PicShop (the "App") is operated by the PicShop developer (an individual developer, "we", "us" or "our"). We understand the importance of your personal information and are committed to protecting it. This policy explains how we collect, use, store, share and protect your personal information, and the rights you have. This policy forms an integral part of the PicShop Terms of Service. Please read it carefully before using the App.

1. Scope and Operator

  • 1.1 Operator: Suyun Lin (an individual developer).
  • 1.2 Contact: selfiearts@163.com. We will respond to your request within 15 business days.
  • 1.3 This policy applies to the PicShop iOS app (including later versions) and related services.
  • 1.4 The App is distributed through the Apple App Store. Apple, as the distribution platform, processes certain information (such as purchase records and crash reports) under its own privacy policy, which is not governed by this policy. We are not Apple, and Apple is not a party to this policy.

2. Personal Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary for the features described below. If you do not provide certain information, the relevant feature may not work, but other features will be unaffected.

  • 2.1 Information you provide
    • (1) Photos: to provide the artistic photo processing service, the App reads photos you actively select or take. We only process the photos you actively select. We do not scan, read or upload the rest of your photo library.
    • (2) Support information: when you contact us by email, we receive the email address, message and attachments you provide.
  • 2.2 Information collected automatically
    • (1) Device and log information: device model, operating system version, app version, language and region settings, IP address, access time, crash and performance logs.
    • (2) Device identifiers: the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) and the Identifier for Vendors (IDFV). IDFA is used for advertising-related purposes only after you grant tracking permission (see Section 6).
    • (3) Subscription status: the "subscription active or not" status provided by Apple. We do not receive your payment card number, billing address or other payment information, which is processed by Apple.
  • 2.3 Information we do not collect
    • We do not collect your contacts, text messages, microphone recordings, precise location, health and fitness data, or the list of other apps installed on your device.

3. How We Use Personal Information

  • 3.1 Providing and improving the service: processing the photos you upload and returning the results; saving results to your photo library (with your permission to add photos); diagnosing crashes, fixing defects and improving performance and compatibility; and measuring feature usage in aggregate and de-identified form to improve the product.
  • 3.2 Advertising: after you grant tracking permission, we and our advertising partners may use advertising identifiers to show you ads and measure their effectiveness. Ads are never targeted based on your photos or their content.
  • 3.3 Subscriptions and transactions: verifying subscription status, restoring purchases and handling refund requests (refunds are handled by Apple under its own policies).
  • 3.4 Security and compliance: preventing fraud and abuse, and responding to lawful requests from judicial or regulatory authorities.
  • 3.5 Purpose limitation: we will not use personal information collected for the purposes above for unrelated purposes. If we need to change the purpose, we will obtain your consent again.

4. Photos and Sensitive Personal Information (Facial Images)

  • 4.1 Photos may contain facial images and other sensitive personal information. We process such photos only with your explicit consent and only to complete the image processing you requested.
  • 4.2 To provide the service, photos are transmitted over an encrypted connection to our servers (or to cloud and technical processing providers acting on our behalf). Once processing is complete and the result has been returned, we delete the server-side copy as soon as possible and in any event within 30 days.
  • 4.3 We do not use your photos to train artificial intelligence models, for advertising, for data analysis unrelated to processing, and we do not sell them or provide them to third parties for those purposes.
  • 4.4 We do not view your photos manually, except where required by law or where you ask our support team to troubleshoot an issue. If manual review is necessary, we will obtain your consent first.
  • 4.5 You must ensure that you have the lawful right to the photos you upload and that any person depicted has consented to their photo being used with this service.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The App itself does not use browser cookies. The App and third-party SDKs may use local storage, SDK identifiers and similar technologies for analytics and advertising. You can manage these settings in iOS: "Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking" and "Settings > Privacy & Security > Apple Advertising". We do not use these technologies for any purpose other than those described in this policy.

6. Advertising and Tracking (App Tracking Transparency)

  • 6.1 Before a feature that requires tracking is first used, we request your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework. Your choice determines whether we may use IDFA to track you across apps and websites.
  • 6.2 Declining permission does not affect the App's core functionality, and you can still use the photo processing service.
  • 6.3 You can turn off "Allow Apps to Request to Track" at any time in "Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking", or limit personalized advertising in "Settings > Privacy & Security > Apple Advertising".
  • 6.4 If tracking is off, you may still see ads, but they will be less relevant.

7. Third-Party Sharing and SDKs

  • 7.1 We do not sell your personal information.
  • 7.2 We do not share your personal information with any company, organization or individual, except in the following cases:
    • (1) with your explicit consent (including ATT permission for ad tracking and system permission for photo access);
    • (2) as necessary to provide a service you requested (for example, cloud service or photo processing providers, which process data only on our instructions);
    • (3) as required by laws and regulations, or in response to lawful requests from judicial or administrative authorities;
    • (4) as necessary to protect the legitimate rights and interests of you, us or the public.
  • 7.3 Third-party SDKs: the App integrates third-party SDKs for advertising, analytics and crash analysis. We require every third party that receives personal information to provide the same or equal protection of user data as described in this policy and to process data only for the purposes and in the manner we have agreed.
  • 7.4 Third-party providers may be located outside your country or region. Cross-border transfer rules are described in Section 10.

8. Storage and Retention

  • 8.1 Location: your personal information is stored on cloud servers we lease. You may ask us for the location of the servers using the contact details at the end of this policy.
  • 8.2 Retention periods:
    • (1) Photos and server-side copies: deleted as soon as possible after processing and return, and in any event within 30 days;
    • (2) Device and log information: no more than 6 months;
    • (3) Advertising identifiers and ad performance data: retained according to the third-party advertising providers' retention rules;
    • (4) Support emails: no more than 12 months after the issue is resolved;
    • (5) where laws or regulations require a different retention period, that period applies.
  • 8.3 After the retention period expires, we delete or anonymize your personal information.

10. Cross-Border Transfers

If your personal information needs to be transferred outside your country or region, we will adopt safeguards required by applicable law (such as security assessments or standard contractual clauses under Article 38 of China's Personal Information Protection Law and Chapter V of the EU GDPR), and will obtain your separate consent where the law requires it.

11. Information Security

We protect your personal information using transport encryption (HTTPS/TLS), access controls, least-privilege principles and periodic purging. However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. In the event of a leak, alteration or loss of personal information, we will notify you and report to the relevant authorities as required by law.
