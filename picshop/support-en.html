Frequently Asked Questions

1. Photo processing failed or is stuck

Please try the following in order:

  1. Check that your internet connection is working (Wi-Fi or cellular), and turn off any proxy or VPN, then try again;
  2. Make sure the photo has not been deleted or moved off the device by "Optimize Storage" — open it once in the Photos app first;
  3. If the photo is very large, crop it in Photos before importing;
  4. Update PicShop to the latest version from the App Store and restart the app;
  5. If the issue persists, email us with the time of processing and a screenshot of any error message.

2. The result does not look as expected

Results vary with lighting, resolution, subject position and algorithm version. For best results, use a clear, well-lit, front-facing photo and avoid backlit, heavily blurred or tightly cropped images. If the result is clearly broken (for example, distorted or not generated at all), send us the original photo and a screenshot of the result so we can investigate.

3. My subscription is not active, or it disappeared after I changed devices

Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID you used to purchase, then tap "Restore Purchases" on the subscription screen in the app. If it still does not restore, send us your purchase receipt from Apple along with the app version number.

4. How to cancel an auto-renewable subscription

  1. Open iOS "Settings";
  2. Tap your Apple ID name at the top;
  3. Tap "Subscriptions";
  4. Select PicShop and tap "Cancel Subscription".

After cancelling, you can continue using the subscription until the end of the current period. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription and does not stop billing.

5. How to request a refund

All payments and refunds are handled by Apple; we cannot issue refunds directly. Please visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with the Apple ID used for the purchase, and submit a refund request. Refund decisions are made by Apple under its own policies.

6. How to delete my photos and personal data

Server-side copies of your photos are deleted automatically within 30 days after processing. To request immediate deletion, or to exercise your rights of access, correction or withdrawal of consent, email selfiearts@163.com and we will respond within 15 business days. See our Privacy Policy for details.

7. Why do I see ads, and how do I turn off personalized ads?

Ads help support some free features of the app. Personalized advertising is only enabled if you allow it in the App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can turn off "Allow Apps to Request to Track" at any time in "Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking", or limit personalized ads in "Settings > Privacy & Security > Apple Advertising". You will still see ads, but they will be less relevant.

8. Which devices and system versions are supported?

The app supports iPhone and iPad. The exact system version requirement is shown on the App Store page. We recommend keeping both iOS and the app up to date for the best experience.

9. Business and press inquiries

Email selfiearts@163.com with "Business" in the subject line.

When contacting us, please include

  • Device model (for example, iPhone 14, iPad Air);
  • iOS version ("Settings > General > About");
  • PicShop version number;
  • The steps you took and what you expected to happen;
  • A screenshot or screen recording of the error or the unexpected result.

Please do not email original photos that contain other people's private or sensitive information. If a photo is needed for troubleshooting, mask unrelated parts first.
